Dal-Rice Khichdi with Banana

Protein Iron Potassium Fiber
1

Pressure cook rice and dal together until they are very soft and mushy.

2

Mash the mixture thoroughly with a clean ladle or masher.

3

Peel and mash a ripe banana separately, then stir into the warm khichdi.

4

Serve lukewarm. The natural sweetness of banana helps babies enjoy the taste.
